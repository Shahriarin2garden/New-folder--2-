Donation Guidelines

  • Fresh & Safe Food Only

    Donate food that is freshly prepared and safe for consumption. Avoid expired or spoiled items.

  • Proper Packaging

    Use clean, airtight containers. Hot food should be kept hot, cold food kept cold during transport.

  • Timely Pickup

    Food should be available for pickup within 4-6 hours of posting to ensure freshness.

  • Clear Information

    Mention food name and any important details to help recipients make informed choices.

Your Impact Matters

Each food donation not only feeds someone in need but also reduces food waste. Together, we're creating a sustainable community where no food goes to waste and no one sleeps hungry.
